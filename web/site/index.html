Bubble Bases

Tap. March. Take every bear.

A two-tap strategy game for iPhone. Grow your army of gummy bears, send them marching across the table and win over every bear before the phone does.

Coming soonon the App Store for iPhone

How to play

1

Tap your bear

Every bear in your colour grows more gummies. The number shows how many it holds.

2

Tap a target

Half your gummies march over. Send more than the target has and that bear joins your team.

3

Take them all

The phone attacks too. Win every bear on the table before it does.

Designed by an 8-year-old

Bubble Bases started as a son's idea: a game where you can take over somebody's territory in a couple of taps. His dad helped build it. The gummy bears, the colours and the next worlds all come from his notebook.
