Privacy Policy

Effective 20 October 2026 · Applies to the Bubble Bases app for iPhone

In short: Bubble Bases does not collect, share or sell any information about you or your children. There are no accounts, no ads and no tracking. Your player name and scores stay on your own phone.

1Who we are

Bubble Bases is a small game made by an independent developer (the seller shown on the App Store page) together with his son. In this policy "we" means that developer.

2Information we collect

None. The app does not send any information to us or to anyone else. Specifically, it does not:

  • ask you to create an account or sign in
  • show advertising or use advertising identifiers
  • include analytics, crash-reporting or tracking tools from other companies
  • use your location, contacts, camera, microphone or photos
  • need an internet connection to play

3What is saved on your phone

The game saves your chosen player name, your settings (colour and difficulty) and your scores so the family scoreboard works between games. This information is stored only inside the app on your device. We cannot see it. Deleting the app deletes it.

4Children

Bubble Bases is made for players of all ages, including children. Because the app collects no personal information from anyone, it collects none from children. A player name typed into the game never leaves the device. We suggest children use a nickname rather than their full name.

5Purchases and future features

If we add optional in-app purchases or Apple Game Center rankings in a later version, those are handled by Apple under Apple's privacy policy. We never receive your payment details. We will update this policy before any such feature is released.

6Your rights

Because we hold no personal data about you, there is nothing for us to access, correct or delete. If you have a question about your privacy, you can contact us at any time.

7Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, we will update this page and the date at the top.
