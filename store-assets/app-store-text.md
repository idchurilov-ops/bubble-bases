**Description:**
Bubble Bases is a two-tap strategy game for the whole family.

Every gummy bear in your colour grows more gummies. Tap one of your bears, then tap another bear: half your gummies march over. Send more than it holds and that bear joins your team. The phone is playing too, so be quick, be clever, and take every bear on the table before it does.

• Learn it in one game, master it over many
• Three levels: Easy, Normal and Hard
• Pick your team colour
• Points for fast wins, with a family scoreboard on your phone
• No ads, no accounts, no data collected
• Works completely offline

Bubble Bases was designed by an 8-year-old and built with his dad.
